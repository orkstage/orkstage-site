Notifications

Who OrkStage notifies about deployments and incidents, through which channels (the console, email, signed webhooks), and what is not available yet.

Who receives what

Notifications go to the tenant's owners and admins and to platform owners and admins. Examples:

  • a deployment started, succeeded or failed;
  • a production deployment is waiting for approval;
  • an instance became degraded, or drift was detected on one of its resources;
  • an incident was opened, updated or resolved.

Each notification in the console links to its deployment, instance or incident. Mark it as read from the bell. The matching API routes are GET /notifications and POST /notifications/:id/read.

Email

The worker queues emails in an outbox and sends them through SMTP (SMTP_HOST, SMTP_PORT, SMTP_SECURE, EMAIL_FROM). It retries a failed email later, up to 5 attempts in total. In the local stack, emails land in Mailpit at http://localhost:8025.

Not yet available

  • Notification preferences per user. The data model stores them, but no screen or API sets them yet.
  • Slack and Teams channels. Until then, point a webhook at a receiver that forwards to them.
