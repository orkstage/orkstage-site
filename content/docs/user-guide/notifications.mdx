## Channels

| Channel | How |
|---|---|
| In the console (the bell in the header) | Always available |
| Email | SMTP or Amazon SES, through an outbox with retries and a per person rate limit |
| [Webhooks](/docs/developer/webhooks/) | HTTP POST of each event as JSON, signed with HMAC |
| Slack | A message to a Slack incoming webhook |
| Microsoft Teams | A message to a Teams incoming webhook or workflow |

Slack and Teams are webhook endpoints with another format. Tenant owners and admins add them under
**Integrations**, choose **Send as** Slack message or Microsoft Teams message, paste the incoming
webhook URL and pick the events. The message carries the title, a short text and a link to the
console. The URL must be one of the service's own hosts (`hooks.slack.com` for Slack;
`webhook.office.com`, `logic.azure.com` or `powerplatform.com` for Teams). These messages are not
signed, because Slack and Teams do not check signatures; the retries, the test button and the
automatic disabling after repeated failures work as for other webhooks.

## Who receives what

Notifications go to the tenant's **owners and admins** and to **platform owners and admins**.
Examples:

- a deployment started, succeeded or failed, or a production deployment waits for approval;
- an instance became degraded, a critical service turned unhealthy, or drift was found;
- an incident was opened, updated or resolved;
- maintenance was scheduled, started or completed;
- a custom domain was verified or could not be verified, its certificate was issued, or it expires
  in 30, 14, 7 or 1 days;
- someone tried to create an instance beyond the tenant's instance limit.

Each notification in the console links to its deployment, instance, incident or page. Mark it as
read from the bell. The matching API routes are `GET /notifications` and
`POST /notifications/:id/read`.

People who follow a status page get their own emails about incidents and maintenance, see
[status pages](/docs/user-guide/status-and-incidents#email-updates).

## Notification settings

**Notification settings** in the console menu lists every event with two switches: **Console**
and **Email**. Everything is on until you turn it off, and your choices apply to you only. The API
is `GET` and `PUT /me/notification-preferences`:

```json title="PUT /me/notification-preferences"
{ "preferences": [{ "event": "deployment.started", "channel": "email", "enabled": false }] }
```

Slack and Teams messages go to channels of the whole tenant, so they are set per endpoint under
Integrations, not per person.

## Email

The worker queues emails in an outbox and retries a failed one later, up to 5 attempts in total.

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `EMAIL_TRANSPORT` | `smtp` (default) or `ses` |
| `SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_SECURE`, `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASSWORD` | The SMTP server, when the transport is `smtp` |
| `EMAIL_FROM` | The sender address; with SES it must be a verified identity |
| `EMAIL_RATE_LIMIT_PER_HOUR` | Emails one person receives per hour at most (default 30); the rest stay in the console |

With `ses`, the worker sends through Amazon SES v2 with its own AWS credentials and `AWS_REGION`,
never with the role of a cloud target. In the local stack, emails land in Mailpit at
http://localhost:8025.
