The marketplace lists templates ready to deploy: complete architectures such as disaster recovery,
a payment gateway, an ecommerce storefront, logistics tracking or last mile delivery. Every
listing is a normal OrkStage template, so it goes through the same placement, plan, policy and
approval checks as any other before anything is created.

## Badges

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Reviewed by platform staff: policies, security defaults and cost. |
| **DevOps reviewed** | Reviewed for operations: backups, health checks, observability and safe defaults. |

Badges are given by reviewers (platform admins and owners) when they approve a listing. A listing
loses its badges when its author edits it, until it is reviewed again. The curated listings that
ship with OrkStage carry both badges.

## Browse and choose

Open **Marketplace** in the console. Search by name, summary or tag, and filter by category,
provider or badge; sort by use, date or name. Each card shows the providers the template can run
on, an approximate monthly cost and how often it was starred and used.

![The template marketplace with search, filters and template cards](/screenshots/marketplace.png)

A listing page shows the description, every resource with its estimated monthly cost, the
placement slots and the providers each one allows, the plans it fits, its triggers and the full
template source.

<Callout title="About the estimate">
  The cost is computed on the first provider each placement slot allows, in a representative
  region, at static list prices. Your own targets, regions and variables change it. The plan review
  before each deployment shows the estimate for your actual choices.
</Callout>

## Use a template

- **Use this template** opens the instance wizard with the template and its published version
  already selected; you choose the tenant, bind each placement slot to your cloud targets and set
  the variables. Templates that need more than one provider require a plan with multiple
  providers (GROWTH or SCALE).
- **Clone and customize** (template authors) copies the template so you can change it in the
  builder and publish your own version.
- **Star** keeps a listing in mind and helps others find popular ones.

## Submit a template

Template authors (the `publish` permission on templates) can submit a template that has a
published version: **Marketplace > Submit a template**, then a slug, title, summary, description,
one to four categories and optional tags. The listing stays hidden until it is reviewed; your
submissions and their review notes are under **My submissions**.

Reviewers see the queue under **Review queue** and approve (with badges), reject or later unlist a
listing. Every submission, edit and review is recorded in the audit log.

## API

The same operations are available under `/api/v1/marketplace/listings` (see the
[API reference](/docs/developer/api-reference/)). Browsing and `use` accept API keys with the
`templates:read` scope; submitting and reviewing need a signed in user.

## Turning the marketplace off

The `TEMPLATE_MARKETPLACE` feature flag (on by default) hides the marketplace for the whole
platform; see [enabling a provider](/docs/providers/#enabling-a-provider) for how flags are
changed.
