Orchestrate every cloud from one stage.

OrkStage deploys and runs your environments on the clouds you choose. Describe an environment once, review its plan, policies and cost, and let OpenTofu apply it while OrkStage watches health, incidents and drift.

How it works

From a template to a running environment.

Nothing touches a cloud until someone reviews the plan, and the worker applies exactly the plan that was approved.

  1. 01

    Describe

    A YAML template states what the environment needs: services, Postgres, cache, queue, storage, ingress and DNS. No provider names inside.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Each resource maps to a concrete cloud service. The plan shows policy results and an approximate monthly cost before anything exists.

  3. 03

    Deploy

    OpenTofu applies the approved plan as a dependency graph, in parallel where it can, with retries. Risky production changes wait for approval.

  4. 04

    Operate

    Health checks, incidents, status pages and drift detection keep watching every instance after the deploy.

Multicloud

Place every resource where it belongs.

Choose placement per resource, not per environment. Put the database on Azure and the rest on AWS, or keep everything in one account. The template does not change.

Providers

Available
AWS, provisioned for real with OpenTofu, and a simulator to try any topology without an account.
Preview
Azure, Google Cloud, OCI, DigitalOcean, Vultr, CoreWeave, Magalu Cloud, Vercel, Render and Supabase, behind feature flags until they are validated on live accounts.

When two resources land on different clouds, the plan shows how they connect: private network, peering, or TLS with an allowlist.

Built for production

Governance and operations come with every instance.

  • Policies on every plan

    Public databases, open storage, missing backups, unapproved regions and budgets are checked before anything is created, and every result is shown.

  • Cost before you deploy

    Every plan carries an approximate monthly cost per resource, from list prices. An estimate, never an invoice.

  • Approvals and audit

    Production deletes, replacements and database changes wait for approval. Every change lands in an audit log that nobody can edit.

  • Health, incidents and status pages

    Alert rules on health and metrics open and resolve incidents. Owners hear about it in the console, by email or by webhook.

  • Drift detection

    Resources changed or deleted outside OrkStage are detected, and the next deploy puts them back the way the template says.

  • Import and automate

    The scan engine turns Terraform, CloudFormation, Kubernetes or Compose into a template. Scoped API keys, signed webhooks and the ork CLI fit your CI.

Get started

Try the whole platform on your machine.

You only need Docker. One Compose command starts the API, worker, console, Postgres, Redis and a local AWS emulator.

Development mode, with the dev login and the Docker socket mounted. Use it to evaluate OrkStage, not in production.
